# Item experience follow-up slices

Owner authorized all four proposed follow-up slices on 2026-10-03, after PR60 combat animation completion. Deliver sequentially: implement, focused replay/invariant tests, full regression, real two-resolution rendering, independent read-only review, PR, exact-head GitHub Codex/actual CI, merge, then begin the next. Each implementation and canonical evidence/memory companion share one PR. Human 30–45 minute play acceptance remains open; scripted journeys do not substitute for it.

1. **PACK-1 — Find and operate held items (L1)**. Classify, search and deterministically sort existing owned items; show quantity and actual total weight; distinguish an empty bag from empty search results; give a direct entry to the inventory section. Equip, unequip, inspect and first-aid actions continue to send existing authoritative intents. Preserve category/search/order after successful actions. Browsing has no world/time/currency/HP/ownership authority. No new slot, item or save field.
2. **GEAR-GUIDE-1 — Sources and pursuit (L1)**. In item details, explain authored ordinary supply locations without promising stock or revealing remote market state. Explain reusable versus consumable items. Unique acquisition information respects actual held items/heard rumors. Correct the mask-to-engineer-tool guidance so players know to switch tracked rumors, with an actual rumor-page entry. Preserve atomic refusal and outcome receipts.
3. **GEAR-BALANCE-1 — A reason to choose each core weapon (L2)**. Resolve the documented ordinary combat-knife disadvantage against the retained hunting knife with one authored stat tradeoff; retain tier/quality semantics and all legacy item identities. Validate real fights and ammunition costs at reviewed skill baselines, explain choice/cost in UI, verify loaded/current tracks and global invariants. No pricing-formula rewrite or random affixes.
4. **AID-1 — One lighter treatment supply (L2)**. The observed gap is a single heavy treatment choice (800g first-aid kit); add one original-library bandage item as a lighter, lower-healing alternative with an explicit price/weight tradeoff. Use the existing settled/out-of-combat treatment authority, exact consumed-item/healed receipt and capacity/ownership checks. Retain old first-aid receipts and saves. No combat buff, injury simulation or extra healing during locked encounters.

Success observation: can the player find what they carry, understand why to equip/use it, buy or pursue the missing requirement, and finish a work/exploration/fight/replenishment journey without guessing hidden rules? Three playtest questions: did you find the needed item quickly; did an equipment difference change your decision; did you know where to go next after obtaining the mask?
