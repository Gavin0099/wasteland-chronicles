It works at those same safe contexts, never changes a turn or pending result. One human companion can coexist; machine consumes no human food/water and grants no party rank or extra cargo capacity.

Existing station turret follows the dog so its immediately preceding shot/turn proof remains valid. Ordinary killing blows, DEFEND/FLEE, empty-ammo rejections and failed/stale commands spend no dog charge. No PURSUIT/named-human attack/capture integration. Support persists across road, field and both authored dungeon contexts, using actual battle/turn/source/room/foe receipts; no standalone extra turn, extra loot/XP/practice award or enemy respawn. Existing ordinary result authority owns outcomes. Preview and attack/shoot labels include exact dog contribution and energy cost before choice; forecast includes finite dog and turret charges where supported.
